# An Electrocardiogram (ECG) signal processing workflow involves cleaning raw electrical data from the heart, extracting key features, and analyzing the waveform to diagnose cardiac conditions.

# Key Components of the ECG Waveform

• P Wave: Represents atrial depolarization (contraction).
• QRS Complex: Represents ventricular depolarization (contraction), serving as the primary marker for heartbeat detection.
• T Wave: Represents ventricular repolarization (relaxation).
• Intervals & Segments: PR, ST, and QT intervals measure conduction timing and help detect ischemia or arrhythmias.
